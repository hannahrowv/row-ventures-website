[ A little about us ]

In your corner,
as you build.

Row Ventures is an independent advisory partner to early-stage companies. We bring financial expertise, strategic thinking and hands-on support to the decisions and work that move a business forward. Finance is our core expertise and the foundation for decisions around growth, spending, hiring, pricing and fundraising.

Row was founded by Sarah Jackson and Hannah Stinson, whose backgrounds span banking and financial services, most recently at Goldman Sachs, where the two met and worked together.

We know the early stages rarely follow a neat plan. Priorities shift, new questions appear and the most important work often sits between functions. Our role is to bring clarity, perspective and momentum where they're needed.
